FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before getting started.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You paste your website URL, and we give you a single line of code to paste into your site. If you can copy and paste, you can run ReplyFox. Most platforms (Shopify, WordPress, Wix) let you paste it into a header setting.

How accurate is the AI?

The chatbot only answers from YOUR content — it won't make up facts about topics outside your knowledge base. It's grounded with retrieval and a low-temperature model, so it's consistent and reliable.

Can I customize how the widget looks?

Yes. You control the widget color, position (bottom-left or bottom-right), greeting message, avatar (emoji or initials), and business hours. A live preview shows your changes instantly.

What happens if it can't answer a question?

It politely asks the visitor for their email so your team can follow up. You'll see every captured lead and the question they asked right in your dashboard — so you both save the sale and learn what to add to your knowledge base.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts. Cancel from the billing page at any time and you won't be billed again. You can keep using the Free plan with 50 messages a month.

Will it slow down my website?

No. The widget loads asynchronously and is under 30KB, so it never blocks your page. It's designed to be conflict-free with your existing site's code.
