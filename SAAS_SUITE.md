## Unified Dashboard Concept

A single login that shows all 3 products:
- Tab 1: Chatbot (ReplyFox) — messages, analytics
- Tab 2: Social (PostPilot) — content calendar
- Tab 3: Products (DescFox) — description history
- Tab 4: Billing — one subscription, one invoice

This makes the suite feel like ONE product (not 3 separate logins). Huge UX advantage over buying 3 different tools from 3 different companies.

## Why This Works

1. **Same customer:** All 3 target small businesses/e-commerce sellers. No new audience to find.
2. **Complementary:** Support + Marketing + Product Copy = complete business toolkit. Not overlapping.
3. **Bundle pricing:** $59 for 3 tools vs $87 separate = obvious value. Customers feel smart choosing the bundle.
4. **One ecosystem:** Customer doesn't need 3 logins, 3 bills, 3 support contacts. Everything in one place.
5. **Switching cost:** Once a business uses all 3, switching away means losing their chatbot + social calendar + product copy history. Very sticky.

**This is how you build a $30k/month business:** not from one product, but from a SUITE of complementary products that share the same customer.
