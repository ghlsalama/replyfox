## Target List (first 50 prospects)

Look for businesses with:
- A website with a "Contact Us" or "FAQ" page
- NO existing live chat widget
- Repetitive customer questions (e-commerce, services, local businesses)
- Active on social media (easier to reach)

Search on Google:
- "[city] [service] business" (e.g., "London plumber", "Manchester dentist")
- "[niche] online store" (e.g., "independent coffee roaster UK")
- "[industry] software company"

Check each result:
1. Do they have a website? ✅
2. Do they have FAQ/Contact pages? ✅
3. Do they have a chatbot already? (if yes, skip)
4. Note their email or contact form URL

Goal: 200 prospects contacted → 10-20 signups (5-10% response rate)

## Pricing Objection Handling

**"It's too expensive"** → "It's less than $1/day. If it saves you even 1 hour/week of answering repetitive emails (worth $15-30/hour), it pays for itself 4x over."

**"I don't need a chatbot"** → "68% of your visitors arrive after hours. Those questions go unanswered. ReplyFox captures them — and captures the visitor's email for follow-up. How many after-hours inquiries are you missing?"

**"Can't I just use ChatGPT?"** → "ChatGPT doesn't know YOUR business. ReplyFox is trained specifically on your website content — your products, your policies, your pricing. And it lives ON your website, available to every visitor automatically."

**"Is the AI accurate?"** → "It only answers from YOUR content. If a question is outside what you've provided, it says 'Let me get your email for follow-up' instead of guessing. Zero hallucinations on topics outside your knowledge base."
